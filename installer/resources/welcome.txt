RTL8852BE development prototype for macOS
By Issen Software Group

This installs:
  • RTL8852BE Wireless Utility.app (includes the prototype kext) → /Applications
  • rtl8852be_cli                                               → /usr/local/bin

Target device IDs: Realtek RTL8852BE 10ec:b852 / 10ec:b85b.
Hardware bring-up is incomplete. This build cannot provide Wi-Fi connectivity.
Kernel startup and the app's loader are blocked. Boot arguments do not bypass
this block. There is no hardware-verified laptop support claim for this build.

App and tools: MIT. Linux-derived hardware code: GPL-2.0-only.
Firmware: Realtek redistribution terms.
Source and audit: https://github.com/AA-EION/RTL8852BE-macOS-WiFi
